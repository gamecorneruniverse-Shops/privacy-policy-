Introduction

Welcome to GCU-SHOPS (https://gcushops.kesug.com), an online retail and digital gaming e-commerce platform operated by Game Corner Universe Limited, established and functioning under the laws of Bangladesh.

We value the trust you place in us when browsing our platform, purchasing gaming gear or digital vouchers, and connecting with our support channels. This Privacy Policy details our operational protocols concerning the collection, storage, and processing of your Personal Data when using our services.

02

Information We Collect

We adhere strictly to the principle of data minimization: we only gather personal data that is directly needed to register your customer profile, process checkouts in Bangladeshi Taka (BDT ৳), organize delivery shipments, and offer after-sales service.

GCU-SHOPS never extracts unauthorized background records or harvests non-essential sensitive information without lawful justification or your explicit authorization.

03

Personal Information

When you interact with GCU-SHOPS, we may collect the following identifiable information:

  • Contact Details: Your full name, active mobile phone number (vital for shipping confirmation & OTP alerts), and email address.
  • Delivery Coordinates: Recipient street address, district, division, postal code, and specific landmarks across Bangladesh.
  • Support Inquiries: Communications shared when messaging our team on WhatsApp or submitting support inquiries.

We do not deliberately collect National ID (NID) numbers or sensitive identity documents during regular retail checkouts unless formally mandated under specific trade regulations in Bangladesh.

04

Account Registration Information

To make shopping faster and retain order histories, customers may register an account. During registration, we collect your name, email, phone number, and account password.

Passwords are cryptographically hashed before being stored in our database. Neither administrators nor staff members have visibility of your raw password. You remain responsible for keeping your credentials safe.

05

Order & Purchase Information

When you complete an order on GCU-SHOPS, we generate and keep transaction-related logs:

  • Order ID numbers, product details, item count, and billing amounts in Bangladeshi Taka (BDT ৳).
  • Order progress statuses, courier consignment numbers, and dispatch records.
  • Warranty registrations, return requests, or replacement tickets filed through our customer portal.
06

Payment Information

🛡️
Strict Payment Security Notice: GCU-SHOPS does NOT record or save credit/debit card numbers (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), CVV/CVC codes, card expiry dates, or personal Mobile Financial Service (MFS) PINs.

All online payments are handled directly by certified third-party payment gateways and mobile financial service providers (such as bKash, Nagad, and Rocket) licensed under Bangladesh Bank guidelines.

  • Your billing details are transmitted through end-to-end encrypted TLS/HTTPS channels directly to the payment gateway.
  • GCU-SHOPS receives solely an automated transaction reference ID (TrxID) and status confirmation (Success/Failed) to finalize your order.
  • For Cash on Delivery (COD) orders, payment is received directly by our logistics courier representative during physical package handover.
07

Cookies & Tracking Technologies

GCU-SHOPS uses standard web browser cookies and session tokens to guarantee reliable performance:

  • Essential Cookies: Retaining items inside your shopping cart, preserving active user logins, and avoiding checkout session drops.
  • Functional Cookies: Storing preferred display settings or recently browsed catalog items.
  • Diagnostics: Evaluating anonymized aggregate visitor counts, loading times, and server performance metrics.

You can clear or block cookies via your browser settings at any point, though doing so might disable certain checkout mechanics.

  • Customer Support: Resolving product questions, handling warranty verification, and assisting via WhatsApp or email.
  • Fraud Prevention: Monitoring checkout sessions for fraudulent activities or fake order placements.
  • Legal & Tax Obligations: Maintaining digital commercial sales ledgers as mandated by the National Board of Revenue (NBR) and domestic trade regulations in Bangladesh.

Game Corner Universe Limited implements technical and organizational security controls to safeguard customer records against unauthorized disclosure or loss:

  • HTTPS / TLS Security: Encrypting active communication sessions between your browser and our store servers.
  • Restricted Access: Limiting backend dashboard access strictly to authorized support and warehouse employees.
  • Continuous Monitoring: Routine software updates and vulnerability checks.
